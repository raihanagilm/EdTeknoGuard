# PRODUCT.md — EdTeknoGuard

## 1. Product Identity & Mission
- **Product Name:** EdTeknoGuard (Admin NOC & Network Telemetry Engine) & TeknoCust (Portal Pelanggan Mandiri).
- **Domain:** ISP Operations, Periodic Automated Optical Network Terminal (ONT) Monitoring, Early Warning System (SOP Optical Attenuation Rx Power dBm), Bandwidth Accounting via MikroTik RouterOS API.
- **Core Mission:** Provide local internet service providers and rural network operators with real-time early detection of optical fiber degradation before link disconnection occurs, paired with self-service modem management for local citizens.

## 2. Target Users & Operating Personas
- **NOC Engineers & Network Admins:** Primary users of TeknoGuard desktop dashboard. Needs dense telemetry data, high scannability, instant diagnostic actions, and zero distracting fluff.
- **Field Technicians:** On-the-go users managing trouble tickets, modem swaps, and field cable splices.
- **Subscribers & Village Residents (TeknoCust):** Self-service mobile portal users checking their network status, reporting issues, and managing home Wi-Fi credentials.

## 3. Surface Modes (Impeccable Taxonomy)
- **TeknoGuard Admin NOC (`/`):** **Mode: `Operate`**
  - High information density, high contrast, clean telemetry metrics, zero decorative AI slop.
  - Prioritizes operational status, live scan triggers, and optical health tables.
- **TeknoCust Citizen Portal (`/portal`):** **Mode: `Operate` / `Persuade`**
  - Mobile-first, friendly, high affordance, straightforward non-technical diagnostics.
